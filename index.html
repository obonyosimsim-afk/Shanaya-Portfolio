GET TO KNOW ME

About Me

Shania Achieng is an Information Communication and Technology specialist holding a Diploma in Information Communication and Technology from the Kenya Institute of Professional Studies (KIPS).

She offers services in network installation, data entry and programming. She is dedicated to her work and believes in delivering quality services while ensuring customer satisfaction.

Beyond her professional work, Shania is passionate about mentoring and empowering upcoming women in technology through initiatives such as Siemens EmpowerHer and the UN African Girls Can Code Initiative.
